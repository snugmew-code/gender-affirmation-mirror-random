You blink at your reflection, your heart fluttering faster than the little pink hearts sparkling around your head.

Wait.

That's you?!

Your tired eyes and messy dark hair are gone, replaced by enormous, glittering pink eyes framed by long, fluttery lashes. Silky, bubblegum-pink hair tumbles down your back in impossibly fluffy twin-tails, bouncing with every tiny movement. Two enormous crimson bows, decorated with golden hearts and delicate white wings, hold your new hairstyle in place. A little heart-shaped tiara rests against your bangs, and an adorable curl springs from the top of your head.

You reach toward the mirror, only to gasp when a dainty, white-gloved hand reaches back.

Your hands!

The gloves are impossibly soft, hugging your slender fingers like a second skin. Frilly white cuffs encircle your delicate wrists, each decorated with a miniature pink bow and a sparkling heart-shaped jewel. You wiggle your fingers, watching the satin catch the light.

"Eeeee! They're so cute!"

And that voice!

High, sweet, bubbly, and unmistakably yours.

Your cheeks turn bright pink.

But your outfit...

Oh, your outfit!

You glance downward, and your heart practically explodes into glitter.

A magnificent pink-and-white magical girl dress hugs your newly petite, feminine figure. Its fitted bodice gently embraces your chest and narrow waist, with delicate pink ribbons crisscrossing down the front like the lacing of a princess's corset. Tiny golden buttons glitter beneath an enormous crimson bow sitting proudly over your heart.

At its center, a magnificent winged pink gemstone pulses with warm, magical light.

You touch it with your gloved fingertips.

Thump-thump!

Your heart answers.

Soft white ruffles frame your shoulders, leaving them deliciously bare, while delicate golden trim shimmers along every edge. A frilly collar tickles your neck, adorned with another ruby-red bow and a dangling golden star.

And your skirt!

You give your hips an experimental wiggle.

Fwip!

Layers upon layers of fluffy pink fabric ripple around your thighs. The short, bell-shaped skirt swishes with a delightful weightlessness, its white petticoats peeking beneath glittering rose-colored ruffles. Every movement sends little waves through the fabric, while oversized bows flutter prettily at your hips.

You can't resist.

You twirl.

Your skirt blossoms around you like a magical flower, your long twin-tails spiraling through the air. The gentle squeeze of your bodice, the brushing of ruffles against your skin, the silky glide of your gloves, and the cheerful jingling of tiny star charms make everything feel wonderfully, impossibly real.

You stop, slightly dizzy.

A little pink heart floats past your nose.

Then another.

And suddenly, you giggle.

Not because it's ridiculous.

Because you're happy.

So unbelievably happy that tears sparkle in your enormous pink eyes.

You clasp your white-gloved hands against your chest, feeling the warm gemstone pulse beneath them.

"By the power of love and friendship..."

Your new voice trembles with excitement.

"...I'll protect everyone's smiles!"

A shower of pink sparkles erupts around you.

Somewhere, a magical mascot is probably preparing a very important speech about destiny, friendship, and defeating the forces of darkness.

But first?

You absolutely need to twirl again.

After all, every magical girl deserves her transformation moment.
