Well, well, well… who is that ravishing creature in your mirror?

You stare, utterly enchanted.

Those luscious silver curls, those sultry crimson eyes, those impossibly pale cheeks and wickedly inviting lips… She's breathtaking. Exactly your type of woman. The sort of dangerously beautiful lady you'd have been far too intimidated to approach.

Except now, darling, she's you.

You run your delicate fingers down your new curves, savoring the delicious pressure of your tightly laced corset. Black lace kisses your porcelain shoulders, burgundy silk embraces your hips, and heavy rubies sway against your slender neck. Your magnificent silver hair tumbles over your generous décolletage in silky, perfumed waves.

You sigh dreamily, exposing two adorable little fangs.

Wait. A vampire with a reflection?

Apparently, this mirror is as fake as your old confidence.

A bashful giggle escapes your glossy lips. You're still a little overwhelmed by how gorgeous you've become.

But then… something exquisite begins blossoming inside your mind.

A delicious warmth spreads through your otherwise icy body. Your timid thoughts soften, unraveling like old ribbons. That familiar urge to hide, to apologize, to make yourself insignificant… how terribly unbecoming!

Something infinitely grander takes its place.

Pride.

Poise.

Power.

You straighten, feeling your spine settle into the effortlessly regal posture of a woman accustomed to being adored. Your hips shift into a graceful, imperious pose. One jeweled hand settles upon your waist while the other rises languidly beneath your chin.

Ohhh… yes.

A rich, velvety laugh bubbles from your throat.

Your mind fills with wonderfully decadent instincts: the proper etiquette of royal courts, the subtle art of seduction, the pleasure of commanding servants with a languid flick of your wrist.

You instinctively tilt your head, batting your long lashes with such exaggerated feminine elegance that you nearly swoon at your own reflection.

"My, my… aren't we simply divine?" you purr.

Your voice is honeyed velvet, dripping with aristocratic superiority. Every syllable feels delicious upon your tongue.

The last remnants of your mortal self melt away beneath a glorious flood of immortal confidence. You no longer merely admire this magnificent vampire countess.

You are her.

And being her feels positively intoxicating.

Why, the very notion that you once worried about the opinions of ordinary humans is hilarious!

You throw back your head, laughing melodically, one hand pressed theatrically against your ample bosom.

"To think I once considered those dreadful little creatures my equals! Oh, how utterly precious!"

Mortals scurry through their pitifully brief lives, imagining themselves important. Such delightful little livestock! Some may become devoted admirers, others obedient servants, and the particularly fortunate might receive the exquisite honor of a countess's kiss.

You blow your reflection a flirtatious kiss.

"Magnificent, darling. Absolutely magnificent."

Suddenly, your sensitive nose detects something dreadful.

Garlic bread.

Oh, the scandal! Your kitchen has become a deathtrap.

You wrinkle your perfect little nose in aristocratic disgust.

Fortunately, another fragrance drifts through the open window.

Warm blood.

Your fangs lengthen. A delicious hunger curls through your belly, making you shiver with wicked anticipation.

Dinner, it seems, is waiting.

You sweep your voluminous skirts aside, offer your reflection one final, extravagantly graceful curtsy, and whisper:

"Do try not to miss me, darling."

A flourish of crimson silk. A cloud of enchanted darkness.

And suddenly, you're a tiny black bat, fluttering into the moonlit night.

How wonderfully convenient.

Even a countess must occasionally dine out.
