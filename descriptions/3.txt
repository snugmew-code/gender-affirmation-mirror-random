Because surely, surely, that impossibly gorgeous creature staring back at you can't actually be you.

And yet...

And that outfit.

Oh, that outfit.

And perched between your new ears? A frilly white maid headdress, perfectly nestled among your luxurious hair.

You turn sideways.

Your fluffy tail swishes behind you, its ribbon and golden bell jingling sweetly.

Jingle.

A delicious shiver runs up your spine.

Everything feels different. Your hair tickles your neck. Your ears pick up distant sounds you've never noticed before. Your tail responds to emotions you haven't even consciously recognized yet.

And your dress...

Every swish, every rustle, every delicate brush of lace against your skin reminds you just how wonderfully feminine you've become.

Actually, you are blushing.

The woman in the mirror looks like trouble.

Beautiful, confident, utterly devastating trouble.

The kind you used to struggle even to look at.

And now?

"Nyaa~?"

Your voice comes out soft, melodic, and dripping with playful sweetness.

You giggle.

Then you lean forward, resting your hands against the sink, deliberately letting your fluffy skirt lift ever so slightly behind you.

Your tail curls into a question mark.

"Welcome home, Master~"

Because underneath all that teasing confidence, beneath the lace, ribbons, bells, and mischievous feline charm, there's something unexpectedly tender.

Relief.

And goodness, you're adorable.
