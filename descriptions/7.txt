Honk! Honk!

Ohhh, what's this?

One moment, you're a sleepy, scruffy nobody staring miserably into your bathroom mirror. The next—

POOF!

A magnificent explosion of pink-and-purple curls erupts from your head! Your hair tumbles into enormous, springy twintails, bouncing wildly as enormous black bows tie themselves into place. Fluffy pom-poms dangle from your curls, tickling your cheeks.

Your glasses disappear. Your lashes flutter. Your lips turn plush and glossy, your cheeks blossom pink, and—

POP!

A shiny red clown nose!

"Heehee!"

Wait. Was that your giggle?

Before you can investigate, your hoodie begins squeezing, shrinking, and reshaping around you.

Rrrrrrip! FWOOOOSH!

Your chest swells into soft, generous curves as a frilly, red-and-cream harlequin bodice wraps itself snugly around your new figure. Its sweetheart neckline plunges daringly, framed by layers of delicate ruffles. The fitted waist hugs you like a corset, making you instinctively wiggle your hips.

"Ooooh~!"

Your voice comes out sweet as candy floss!

Puffy sleeves blossom around your shoulders, leaving your silky skin deliciously bare. A frilly collar flutters against your neck. Two ridiculously fluffy pom-poms bounce against your bodice with every tiny movement.

Then your trousers transform.

FWOOMP!

A scandalously short, flouncy clown skirt explodes around your hips!

"Oop! Heeheehee!"

You instinctively grab the hem, only to discover gorgeous diamond-patterned thigh-high stockings hugging your newly shapely legs. Little crimson bows decorate your thighs, and suspender straps stretch teasingly beneath your ruffled skirt.

Every movement brings a delightful new sensation: stockings sliding against soft skin, snug straps giving gentle little tugs, puffy sleeves brushing your arms, and that absurdly tiny skirt swishing whenever your hips sway.

And sway they do.

A little party hat pops onto your head.

Ding-a-ling!

Golden star earrings swing beside your face.

You blink.

Then blink again.

And suddenly—

"BAHAHAHA! OH, LOOK AT MEEEE!"

Your hands fly to your cheeks.

That wasn't just a laugh. That was a full-blown, shameless, theatrical clown-girl CACKLE!

Something wonderfully silly is fizzing through your head. All those years of worrying what people might think? Pbbbbbt! Gone! The anxious little voice telling you to behave? Squashed flat beneath an imaginary oversized clown shoe!

You stick out your tongue.

"Heehee! Ooh, what a naughty little clownette!"

You bend toward the mirror, deliberately giving your curls a dramatic bounce. Your ruffles flutter, your pom-poms bob, and your new figure looks absolutely spectacular in its cheeky little circus costume.

"Mwah!"

You blow your reflection a kiss.

Then you boop your nose.

HONK!

Again.

HONK-HONK!

"Ahahaha! I'm ADORABLE!"

You twirl, squeal, nearly trip over your own feet, and recover with a ridiculously exaggerated curtsy.

Somehow, every silly gesture feels perfectly natural. The giggles, the flirting, the cheeky little winks, the irresistible urge to cause harmless chaos... it's almost like you've spent your entire life rehearsing this act.

You lean forward, batting your eyelashes.

"Well, well, well! Who's the prettiest little prankster in the whole circus?"

Your reflection grins.

"ME, silly!"

You throw your arms wide, strike a dazzling pose, and announce to an imaginary audience:

"LADIES AND GENTLEMEN! YOUR FAVORITE FLIRTY LITTLE CLOWNETTE HAS ARRIVED!"

Honk! Honk!

And goodness gracious, she's going to be an absolute menace.
