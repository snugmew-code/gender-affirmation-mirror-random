One moment, you're staring miserably into your bathroom mirror, wondering if your thirties are supposed to feel this exhausting.

The next, a suspiciously saucer-shaped UFO appears outside your window.

There's a low, otherworldly hum, an intense pink flash, and—

ZAP!

"E-EEEEH?!"

You stumble backward, nearly knocking over your toothbrush.

There's a girl in your bathroom.

A ridiculously cute, green-skinned alien girl!

And she's copying your every movement!

Your eyes widen. Hers widen too, revealing enormous, sparkling violet irises. You poke your cheek. She pokes hers. You squeeze your face between your hands, discovering wonderfully smooth, soft green skin and the most adorable little pointed ears.

Oh.

OH.

You're HER?!

Your heart practically performs a hyperspace jump.

Your reflection is absolutely precious! Your silvery-white hair is arranged into two enormous, fluffy buns, with delicate curls framing your face. Tiny star-shaped clips sparkle among your bangs, while dangling planetary earrings sway whenever you move.

And your outfit!

You're wearing a glossy, pearlescent lavender space uniform, complete with sleek detached sleeves, a high collar, and a short, wonderfully swishy skirt decorated with little planets. Around your waist sits a futuristic utility belt, its glowing pink power cells humming softly against your hips.

Even your fingernails are perfectly manicured!

You give your skirt an experimental twirl.

Swish!

"Hehehe..."

That giggle was yours?!

You cover your mouth, blushing greenish-pink.

Curiosity gets the better of you, and you glance down to inspect the rest of yourself.

Your newly shapely green legs end in a pair of gorgeous, knee-high, pearly-white space boots! Their chunky platforms make you pleasantly taller, while glowing lavender seams trace their smooth surfaces. You lift one foot experimentally, admiring the little metallic stars decorating your ankles.

They're surprisingly lightweight! Your toes wiggle comfortably inside, and a soft magnetic hum accompanies every step.

You bounce on your heels, making your skirt flutter.

You feel so light! So energetic! So unbelievably cute!

Before you can properly appreciate this frankly miraculous development, something tickles your scalp.

"Huh?"

Two little bumps push through your fluffy white hair.

You reach up, feeling them squirm beneath your fingertips.

"Eeep?! W-What's growing?!"

The bumps swell, pushing apart your silky locks. Something slender and surprisingly sensitive begins emerging from each one, stretching upward like a pair of tiny green sprouts.

You gasp as they lengthen, sending peculiar tingles through your scalp. They're not painful, but you can actually feel them growing!

Another centimeter. Then another.

The tips thicken into delicate, translucent bulbs, while the stalks curve gracefully above your head.

You cautiously touch one.

"Ahhn! T-That tickles!"

Both antennae immediately curl backward.

Wait.

You can MOVE them?!

You stare, cross-eyed, as you concentrate, and your new appendages straighten obediently. You wiggle them left, then right, watching in fascination as they sway independently.
Suddenly, both antennae flare brilliantly.
An unfamiliar pressure builds behind your eyes.

Then—

Vmmmm...!

A strange, overwhelming sensation floods your skull.

Your smile freezes.

For approximately half a second, your head feels like a computer installing several billion years of extraterrestrial civilization. Your understanding of the universe rearranges itself. Strange languages become effortless. Alien customs feel instinctive. Your thoughts accelerate, growing brighter, quicker, almost impossibly enthusiastic.

Then the installation finishes.

Your expression brightens.

"Aaaah! There we go! Heehee!"

You wiggle your antennae happily.

Of course! You're Zizi, a twenty-something star-cycle-old Virellian girl from the lovely violet oceans of planet Virellia-7!

Your people are famous throughout the Andromeda sector for their advanced technology, irresistible cuteness, and occasionally questionable definitions of peaceful diplomacy.

And you're an official Junior Planetary Evaluation Officer!

How exciting!

You look around the strange little room. Such primitive plumbing! Such inefficient lighting! And what's with that enormous porcelain bowl? Do humans seriously dispose of perfectly recyclable biological material by flushing it away with drinking water?!

"Uwaaah... Earthlings really are weird!"

You reach toward your hip, where your trusty plasma blaster rests in its magnetic holster.

"Ah! Pew-Pew Mk. IV! There you are, sweetie!"

The little pink-and-silver weapon chirps affectionately when you pick it up.

Naturally, you know exactly how to operate it. Its settings include stun, disintegrate, molecular rearrangement, and low-power specimen immobilization.

Very useful for field research!

You strike a pose before the mirror, holding your blaster beside your face and flashing a peace sign.

"Vii-vii! Galactic cuteness patrol!"

Perfect!

Your antennae flash enthusiastically.

Now, what was your assignment again?

Oh, right!

You arrived with the Virellian Expeditionary Fleet to determine humanity's future.

Your adorable little civilization has three official recommendations: enslavement, extermination, or conversion into loyal Virellian citizens.

Such a difficult decision!

You puff your cheeks thoughtfully.

"Mmmm... Enslavement would be soooo much paperwork!"

Your antennae droop.

"And extermination? Ugh! That would be, like, super sad!"

You peer through the window toward the sleeping human city.

Earthlings really are rather cute, despite their embarrassing lack of antennae.

Especially the males!

You remember that big, muscular specimen your crew abducted for probing last week.

Ooooh, he was fascinating!

Such broad shoulders! Such strong arms! And those wonderfully developed pectoral muscles!

Just remembering how he flexed against the examination restraints makes a strange warmth spread through your chest.

"Ehehe... Maybe we should bring him back for some... additional research?"

Your antennae curl into a little heart.

Ahem!

Professionalism, Zizi! Professionalism!

Maybe conversion would be best!

A few million transformation beams, some personality reprogramming, and voilà! A whole planet of adorable new Virellians!

Although...

Wouldn't it be a shame to convert all those handsome human males?

You thoughtfully tap your chin with your blaster.

"Hmmm... Perhaps we should establish a protected breeding population for scientific observation!"

There! A perfectly reasonable proposal!

Your communicator chirps.

"Scout Zizi! Report your findings!"

You snap to attention, antennae standing perfectly straight.

"Zizi reporting! Earth is super primitive, but its inhabitants are totally adorable! Recommend planetary conversion with limited specimen preservation!"

You beam proudly.

Another successful day of intergalactic diplomacy!
