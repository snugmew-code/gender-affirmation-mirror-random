Ohohohoho! My, What a Lovely Lady You Are!

You simply cannot stop admiring yourself.

And really, who could possibly blame you?

Your reflection is exquisite.

Those magnificent golden ringlets cascade past your shoulders in enormous, perfectly sculpted drills. Their silky weight caresses your neck, every luxurious curl bouncing and swaying with even the slightest movement.

Ahh~ Such glorious hair! Surely lesser ladies would weep with envy!

Your jeweled tiara rests upon your head like the crown you so naturally deserve. Ruby earrings sway against your delicate neck, their cool, precious weight sending delicious little tingles down your skin.

And your dress! Oh, your darling dress!

Burgundy velvet embraces your elegant figure, its fitted corset holding your waist in the most exquisitely feminine silhouette. Every breath makes you wonderfully aware of its snug embrace. Layers of ivory lace flutter against your skin, their softness positively divine. Golden embroidery, enormous ribbons, glittering gemstones...

My goodness! What an absolute triumph of aristocratic beauty!

Your white silk gloves glide sensuously over your delicate fingers.

Such dainty, refined fingers!

You raise one toward your cheek, savoring the silky brush against your soft skin.

"My, my~ How positively divine!"

Your voice emerges sweet and melodious, every syllable dripping with the most exquisite refinement.

Naturally, you sound lovely. Why wouldn't you?

You tilt your chin, sending those magnificent golden drills bouncing.

Modesty? Oh, how terribly common!

A lady blessed with such extraordinary beauty and impeccable breeding has a duty to display her superiority!

Indeed, imagining some ordinary commoner daring to address you as an equal makes your delicate nose wrinkle.

"Honestly! Such appalling manners! Do these people receive no education whatsoever?"

You pout adorably.

Oh, how satisfying!

Your standards are simply impeccable. Cheap clothing? Revolting! Instant coffee? Barbaric! Anyone forgetting their proper place in your presence?

Absolutely unforgivable!

You deserve afternoon tea in the finest porcelain, exquisite pastries arranged upon silver trays, and obedient servants who understand that your slightest preference is of the utmost importance.

Naturally, conversations should revolve around your magnificent beauty, exquisite taste, and countless accomplishments.

What else could possibly be worth discussing?

You place one gloved hand upon your hip, arching your back with effortless aristocratic poise. Your other hand rises gracefully toward your cheek.

Ohhh~ The delicious weight of your enormous curls! The gentle pressure of your corset! The luxurious rustling of your skirts!

Every little movement reminds you just how wonderfully, overwhelmingly ladylike you are!

And goodness, how you adore the spotlight!

A deliciously wicked thought crosses your mind.

Perhaps you should make someone apologize for something trivial.

Just to hear them grovel.

How amusing!

After all, a little humiliation might teach those unfortunate souls some much-needed manners!

Your lips curl into the sweetest, most delightfully condescending smile.

Oh, how charitable of you!

Then something bubbles up inside your chest.

A light, musical giggle.

"Fu... fufufu..."

Your shoulders tremble. Your ringlets bounce.

And suddenly—

"OHOHOHOHOHO!"

Your exquisite laughter fills the bathroom as you throw your head back, one gloved hand elegantly covering your mouth.

Oh, that feels marvelous!

Such impeccable elegance! Such exquisite refinement! Such an extraordinary combination of beauty, breeding, and sophistication!

Truly, you are everything a proper young lady ought to be!

And what a wonderful privilege it must be for others to bask in your magnificent presence!

"Ohohohoho! Now then, I believe it's time for tea! Do have the maid prepare the drawing room. And kindly remind her that a lady must never be kept waiting!"

You smile sweetly at your reflection.

And should your tea arrive even a moment late?

Well!

Someone shall have a most educational afternoon.

"Ohohohohoho!"
