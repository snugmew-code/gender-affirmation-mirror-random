You stare into the bathroom mirror, expecting the same tired, miserable face you've seen for thirty-something years.

Instead, your reflection winks.

Wait. Winks?

A strange warmth blossoms in your chest, spreading down your arms and legs like liquid magic. Your shoulders narrow, your waist pulls inward, and your entire body tingles as it rearranges itself beneath your clothes. Your skin grows wonderfully smooth, your cheeks soften, and your lips plump into a pretty little pout.

A startled squeak escapes your throat.

Oh. That's your voice?

It's sweet, feminine, and adorably high-pitched!

Your glasses slip down your shrinking nose before disappearing in a puff of purple smoke. Suddenly, you can see perfectly. Even better, you can see the enormous violet eyes blinking back at you, framed by thick lashes.

Then comes your hair.

Soft, silky strands cascade past your shoulders, spilling down your back in luxurious waves of midnight purple. You giggle as they tickle your bare skin, watching two thick braids weave themselves into existence, tied with tiny lavender bows.

Your hoodie dissolves into sparkling mist.

In its place, a frilly cream blouse hugs your new figure, its off-shoulder sleeves exposing delicate collarbones. A tightly laced black corset cinches your waist, while layers of purple fabric flutter around your thighs.

A velvet choker clasps itself around your neck. Golden crescent earrings dangle against your cheeks. Rings sparkle across your slender fingers, and tiny potion bottles jingle at your hips.

Finally, an enormous, floppy witch's hat materializes atop your head.

You stare.

Then you smile.

And something clicks.

Your memories begin shifting, rearranging, expanding. Forgotten incantations dance across your thoughts. Ancient rituals become as familiar as brushing your teeth. You suddenly know seventeen ways to brew a love potion, nine methods of breaking curses, and precisely which mushrooms make the most delightful poisons.

You also remember something particularly important.

You're an excellent witch.

"Hehehe..."

Your giggle sounds different now. Not nervous. Not apologetic.

Dangerous.

With a lazy flick of your wrist, you summon a broomstick in a shower of violet sparks. It lands neatly in your waiting hand, decorated with ribbons and golden charms.

Naturally. Why walk when you can fly?

You tilt your enormous hat, flashing your reflection a wicked little grin. Your violet eyes glitter with trouble, and one tiny fang peeks between your lips.

Gone is the timid little doormat who couldn't even complain about a wrong coffee order.

Now?

You make the rules.

And any foolish boy who thinks a cute face means a harmless witch is in for a terrible surprise.

One wrong remark, and he'll be croaking in a pond.

Or perhaps purring on your lap.

You do adore black cats, after all.

With a smug little "Nyehehe~," you blow your reflection a kiss.

Careful, world. You've got a new witch to worry about.
