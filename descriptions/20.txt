“Oh, my stars and garters!”

The gasp escapes your perfectly painted lips before you can even think to stop it. One gloved hand flies to your mouth, the other pressing dramatically against your fluttering heart. Good heavens! Just look at you!

The beautiful blonde in the mirror blinks her enormous blue eyes, her rosy cheeks growing positively scarlet. And when you blink, she blinks too!

Why, that darling little lady is you!

Your heart skips a beat beneath layers of delicate lace and powder-blue satin. Your new figure feels wonderfully different: a dainty waist hugged snugly by a fitted corset, soft curves filling out your elaborate bodice, and delicate shoulders peeking from beneath enormous ruffled sleeves. Your magnificent skirts swish around you with the slightest movement, their luxurious weight making every step feel like a grand entrance at a debutante ball.

Beneath all that satin, layers of fluffy, starched petticoats rustle against an enormous, bell-shaped hoopskirt, its springy crinoline framework keeping your skirts perfectly puffed out. And peeking beneath the hem? The daintiest ivory button-up boots, their little heels clicking sweetly against the floor!

And your hair! Mercy!

Golden ringlets tumble past your shoulders in impossibly soft, bouncy cascades. You timidly touch one, squeaking when it springs back against your cheek. Your enormous, wide-brimmed picture hat sits atop those gorgeous curls, lavishly decorated with magnolia blossoms, delicate lace, and enormous powder-blue satin bows. Its extravagant brim frames your pretty face beautifully, bobbing ever so slightly whenever you tilt your head.

Your dangling sapphire earrings sway gently, their cool weight brushing your neck. A dainty cameo necklace rests against your throat, while elegant white gloves hug your slender fingers like a second skin.

"Oh, sugar, ain't you just the prettiest little thing!"

You freeze.

Was that your voice?

That sweet, lilting Southern drawl flows from your lips as naturally as honey from a spoon!

"Well, I do declare!"

Another gasp. Another blush. Another flutter of your eyelashes.

And goodness gracious, how natural it all feels!

You instinctively straighten your posture, delicately clasping your gloved hands before your waist. Your shoulders settle, your chin tilts, and your lips blossom into the sweetest, most ladylike smile imaginable.

Already, your thoughts are filling with the loveliest things.

Magnolia blossoms! Sweet tea on a shady veranda! Lace parasols, garden parties, handwritten invitations, and handsome gentlemen tipping their hats!

The thought of wearing anything less elegant than your gorgeous gown suddenly seems downright scandalous.

And speaking without proper manners? Perish the thought!

"Oh, bless your heart," you murmur, practicing a tiny, affectionate smile.

My goodness, you're practically made for this!

You give your skirts an experimental swish, listening to your petticoats whisper and feeling your hoops bounce beneath the billowing fabric. Then comes a delicate little curtsy, followed by a bashful giggle that makes your earrings dance.

"Heavens to Betsy! Whatever shall I do with myself?"

But you already know.

There are afternoon teas to host, compliments to graciously accept, flowers to arrange, and an entire world waiting to meet its newest Southern sweetheart.

You blow your reflection a dainty little kiss.

"Well, aren't you just precious, darlin'?"

And with one last delighted flutter of your lashes, you decide that a proper Southern lady simply must celebrate.

Sweet tea, anyone?
