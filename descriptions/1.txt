You blink at the mirror.

Once. Twice.

Your reflection blinks back, enormous violet-blue eyes widening in disbelief. Your lips part, releasing a tiny, breathless squeak that sounds so impossibly sweet you almost don't recognize it as your own.

"W-Wha...? Is that really me?"

Your voice is soft, light, musical. You gasp again just to hear it.

A cascade of golden curls tumbles over your shoulders, tickling your bare skin with every little movement. Your cheeks are wonderfully warm, a rosy blush spreading across your delicate face. You lift one slender hand to your mouth, feeling the unfamiliar softness of your lips beneath your fingertips.

And then you look down.

Oh.

Oh, goodness.

Your dress.

Your beautiful, beautiful dress.

A magnificent lavender ballgown embraces your new figure, its fitted bodice hugging your narrow waist with a gentle, reassuring snugness. Delicate golden embroidery winds across the silky fabric, glimmering whenever you move. Tiny ribbons crisscross your corset, while an enormous purple bow rests proudly against your chest.

You inhale sharply, feeling the bodice rise with your breath. The snug fabric presses softly against your newly feminine curves, and the sensation sends a delightful little shiver down your spine.

Your shoulders are bare, decorated with the most adorable puffed sleeves imaginable. Layers of white lace flutter around your upper arms, their delicate edges brushing your skin like little kisses.

And your skirt!

You instinctively reach down, gathering handfuls of impossibly soft fabric. Lavender silk slips between your fingers, followed by layers upon layers of fluffy tulle, embroidered ruffles, and delicate lace.

You give your hips an experimental wiggle.

The entire gown swishes.

You freeze.

Then wiggle again.

Swish, swish.

A helpless giggle escapes your lips.

"Hehehe...!"

You straighten, sending your golden curls bouncing. Something pleasantly heavy rests atop your head: a magnificent golden tiara, its sparkling amethysts perfectly matching your dangling earrings and elegant necklace.

You touch the crown carefully, almost afraid it might vanish.

But it's real.

The gentle weight of your jewelry. The ticklish lace against your arms. The unfamiliar sway of your curls. The wonderful fullness of your skirt surrounding you like a lavender cloud.

All real.

Your eyes grow misty.

How many times had you imagined something like this? How often had you lingered over illustrations of beautiful princesses, secretly wondering what it might feel like to wear their dresses, to look into a mirror and see someone so breathtakingly lovely?

And now...

You slowly turn toward the mirror again.

A gorgeous, blushing princess stares back.

You.

Your lips tremble into the biggest, happiest smile you've ever worn.

"I'm... I'm a princess...!"

You lift your skirt and attempt a tiny, clumsy curtsy, immediately dissolving into delighted giggles as your countless ruffles bounce around you.

For once, you don't want to look away from your reflection.

You want to keep looking.

Forever, if you could.

Because the girl in the mirror is finally you.
