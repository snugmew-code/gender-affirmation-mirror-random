Who will you become?

You stare into the mirror, tired of who you see. The same messy hair, the same exhausted eyes, the same face you've looked at for years. You sigh, leaning against the sink. What if things were different? You close your eyes, imagining a girl staring back instead. What would she look like? Would she be cute? Beautiful? Maybe sweet and innocent, or bold and rebellious? Whatever she'd look like, she'd be someone you could finally smile at in the mirror. For a moment, you let yourself get lost in the fantasy. If only I could be her... You open your eyes. Something feels... different. The mirror's surface begins to shimmer with a faint pink glow. Your reflection flickers, its features blurring like ripples in water. Then, a soft, feminine voice whispers from somewhere inside the glass: "Well then... let's see who you really want to be~ ♡" And suddenly, your reflection begins to change...
